## NASA Solves How a Jupiter Jet Stream Shifts into Reverse

<p class="text-muted">NASA Goddard · December 18, 2017</p>

<div class="row mt-3">
  <div class="col-md-7 mb-3">
    <div class="ratio ratio-16x9" style="position:relative;padding-bottom:56.25%;height:0;overflow:hidden;border-radius:8px;">
      <iframe style="position:absolute;top:0;left:0;width:100%;height:100%;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/4tJkhPmMAl4" title="NASA: Jupiter jet stream reversal" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col-md-5">
    <p>Using five years of temperature observations from NASA's Infrared Telescope Facility together with atmospheric modeling, our team found that gravity waves generated by convection in Jupiter's lower atmosphere drive the periodic reversals of its equatorial jet stream, Jupiter's version of Earth's Quasi-Biennial Oscillation. I was the lead author of the study.</p>

## Jupiter's Great Red Spot Getting Taller as It Shrinks

<p class="text-muted">NASA Goddard · March 13, 2018</p>

<div class="row mt-3">
  <div class="col-md-7 mb-3">
    <div class="ratio ratio-16x9" style="position:relative;padding-bottom:56.25%;height:0;overflow:hidden;border-radius:8px;">
      <iframe style="position:absolute;top:0;left:0;width:100%;height:100%;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/JDi4IdtvDVE" title="NASA: Great Red Spot getting taller" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col-md-5">
    <p>Combining historical telescope records, spacecraft data and Hubble observations, our team found that the Great Red Spot has been shrinking for about 150 years while growing taller, and that its westward drift has recently sped up. I was a co-author and was quoted on what the coming decade could bring:</p>
    <blockquote class="blockquote"><p>"If the trends we see in the Great Red Spot continue, the next five to 10 years could be very interesting."</p></blockquote>
    <p>
      <a class="btn btn-sm btn-outline-primary" href="https://www.nasa.gov/missions/jupiters-great-red-spot-getting-taller-as-it-shrinks-nasa-team-finds/">Read the NASA feature</a>

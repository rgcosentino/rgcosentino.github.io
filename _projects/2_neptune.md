Through my ALMA program *Neptune's High-Altitude Wind Chill* (2022.1.00548.S), I obtained the highest spatial resolution observations of Neptune's upper atmosphere ever made. Beyond measuring the planet's stratospheric winds and vertical wind shear, the data reveal spatial correlations between trace-gas enhancements and storm activity.

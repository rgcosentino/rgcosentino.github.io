As PI of the ALMA program *Monitoring Giant Planet High-Altitude Jet Streams* (Cycles 9, 10, 11 and beyond), I'm building the first multi-year, high-cadence record of stratospheric winds on Jupiter and Saturn using the ALMA Compact Array. Doppler shifts of molecular lines such as CO and HCN reveal how the high-altitude jets change from season to season, a first step toward a true "climate record" for planets beyond Earth.

**Open tools.** The wind retrievals use an open-source, planet-agnostic Doppler wind toolkit I'm developing in [pylanetary](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7608476) ([my fork](https://github.com/rgcosentino/pylanetary_RGC)), so measurements are reproducible across planets.

Uranus' extreme 98° obliquity gives it the most extreme seasons in the Solar System. As the planet approaches northern summer solstice around 2030, my ALMA program *Uranus' Polar Bullseye* (2024.1.00104.S) is monitoring the north polar region to follow the evolution of its polar cyclone, temperature structure and trace gases.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/proj_uranus.jpg" title="Uranus (illustration)" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

These measurements will constrain models of ice-giant seasonal circulation and support planning for a future Uranus mission (see [Dahl et al. 2024](/publications/)).

What began as a conversation at a summer intern poster session became a new line of research: using ALMA to measure Titan's winds at high cadence. With Martin Cordiner and colleagues, we found a remarkable drop in the speed of Titan's equatorial super-rotating jet over a short time, consistent with barotropic instability ([Cordiner et al. 2020, ApJL](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/abc688)). The Doppler wind and spectral-line toolkit developed for this work now underpins my studies of Jupiter, Saturn and Neptune, and we've continued monitoring Titan through its 2025 equinox.

Moving Solar System objects will streak through Roman's wide-field survey images. Rather than treating them only as contaminants, I'm developing ways to identify them so they can be removed from cosmology datasets *and* used for science: catalogs of small bodies, studies of dynamical families, and planetary defense ([Holler, Cosentino et al. 2025, PASP](/publications/)). This work is supported by the STScI internal project *Asteroids are not all Cosmic Rays for Roman*, in collaboration with Bryan Holler.
